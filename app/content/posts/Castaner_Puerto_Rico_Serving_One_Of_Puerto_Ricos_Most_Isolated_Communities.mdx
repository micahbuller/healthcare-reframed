showNotes: "This episode explores healthcare delivery in the mountain community of Castañer, Puerto Rico. Giovanni Ortiz Pagán describes a service area marked by poverty, difficult roads, dozens of bridges, limited transportation, daily rain, and long travel times from metropolitan centers. He explains how Hospital General Castañer uses housing, transportation, clinician support, and outreach to recruit and retain staff. Dr. José Rodríguez-Ramos adds the perspective of a family physician who has served the community for decades, describing broad-scope rural medicine, teleconsultation challenges, limited broadband access, physician pay disparities, and the need to train future family physicians through rural pathways in Puerto Rico. The conversation also reflects on Hurricane Maria and how teams provided home-based care when infrastructure and communication failed."
